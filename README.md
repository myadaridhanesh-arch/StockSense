StockSense

        StockSense is a centralized, modular Inventory Management System designed to replace manual registers, Excel sheets, and fragmented stock tracking with a simple, real-time digital workflow. Built with a modern React-based interface and Vite setup, the system provides a unified platform for inventory managers and warehouse staff to manage products, locations, receipts, deliveries, internal transfers, stock adjustments, and movement history. The frontend uses React with Tailwind CSS and Lucide icons for a clean, responsive interface, while the application can be served locally through the included Python server.
        
        StockSense turns every stock movement into a traceable operation. When goods arrive, a Receipt automatically increases stock; when goods are dispatched, a Delivery Order decreases stock; Internal Transfers move stock between locations without changing the organization's total inventory; and Stock Adjustments reconcile system quantities with physical counts. Each operation is recorded in a Move History/Stock Ledger, creating a clear trail of how inventory changed.

        For example, in our working demonstration, 100 KG of Steel Rod is received into the Main Store. Then 30 KG is transferred to the Production Floor, keeping total stock at 100 KG while updating the location-wise quantities. Next, 20 KG is delivered, reducing total stock to 80 KG. Finally, a physical count identifies 3 KG of damaged stock, resulting in a final inventory of 77 KG. All four operations—Receipt, Internal Transfer, Delivery, and Adjustment—are recorded in the ledger, and the complete 17-step demo validation passes successfully.

        The solution also provides product and SKU management, location-wise stock visibility, reorder-level tracking, smart filtering, multi-location inventory movement, dashboard insights, and operational history. This transforms inventory management from a reactive record-keeping task into a structured, transparent, and easy-to-monitor process.

        In short, StockSense gives businesses one reliable source of truth for their inventory—from receiving goods to moving, delivering, counting, and reconciling them—reducing manual effort, improving stock visibility, and making every inventory movement accountable.
